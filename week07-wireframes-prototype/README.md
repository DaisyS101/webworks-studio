## 2. Three Design Requirements
Translate your **top three Week 6 priorities** into exactly three interface requirements.

### Requirement 1
**Week 6 problem:** Maya would have diffulties finding which trail fits her 
**Design requirement:**  has to both pop out so it is easy find while also not be so distracting or in the way of the rest of the page
**How this helps Maya:**  This helps by narrowing down the list of the trails so it can narrow the list of options without becoming overwhelmed or scanning every trail herself.

### Requirement 2
**Week 6 problem:**  Lack of Trail Card Time Durations
**Design requirement:**  Each trail should show how much time it takes or at least the estimation so it can be compared to her time limit that she wishes to go for.
**How this helps Maya:**  this helps Maya take charge and choose herself on how much time she wants to spend on the hiking trail and how much she should prepare for said trail as it can be dangerous to go unprepared

### Requirement 3
**Week 6 problem:**  Diffiuclties in assigning labels since its important they are properly labeled. otherwise it wont be known if it is easy as taking a walk or just extremely hard.
**Design requirement:**  Has to expalin it simply dont over complicate it or use words that would be confusing.
**How this helps Maya:**  can help decide whether or not if its easy or just plain hard and difficult to complete. if its easy and feels like a beginner can do the trail in 3 hours or less! if not well time to rethink the path then and find a new terrain that can host the hike.
